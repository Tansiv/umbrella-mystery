Character Bios – The Umbrella in the Rain: A Diary of Secrets
=============================================================

1. Maya Arwen
--------------
Role: Protagonist  
Age: 17  
Bio: Quiet, observant, and emotionally fractured since the death of her brother Elias. She has a habit of writing things down backwards in her notebook—a defense mechanism that started after the funeral.  
Secret: She once received a voicemail from Elias, timestamped *two days after* his reported drowning. She has never told anyone.

---

2. Elias Arwen
--------------
Role: Maya’s older brother  
Age: 19 (deceased?)  
Bio: Sensitive and artistic, Elias was obsessed with riddles and rain. He vanished after a river accident during a storm. Everyone assumes he drowned—except Maya.  
Secret: Pages from his diary keep surfacing in impossible places, some written in a language he never knew.

---

3. Mrs. Elara Lera
------------------
Role: Literature teacher at Rowan School  
Age: 42  
Bio: A widow with a gentle smile and a hidden sadness. She taught both Maya and Elias. Her classroom always smells of old paper and jasmine.  
Secret: She keeps a locked box in her drawer labeled *“R.L. - Witness”*. The key is sewn inside her coat lining.

---

4. The Man in the Hat
----------------------
Role: Unknown  
Age: Unknown  
Bio: Appears only during rainstorms. Tall, with a wide-brimmed black hat that obscures his face. Maya saw him twice—once at the bridge, once in her dreams.  
Secret: A photo of him was found inside Elias’s diary, but it was printed using outdated 1970s film stock.

---

5. Camellia “Cam” Brine
------------------------
Role: Maya’s only friend  
Age: 17  
Bio: Loud, brash, and loyal. Camellia loves photography and has a knack for noticing things others miss. Her lens often captures what the eye cannot.  
Secret: She once accidentally photographed the Man in the Hat—and the image turned blurry around his figure, even on film.

---

6. Principal Thorne Haxley
---------------------------
Role: Principal of Rowan School  
Age: 55  
Bio: Stern, formal, and always carries an umbrella, even when it’s dry. He speaks in short, clipped sentences and is rarely seen without his gloves.  
Secret: He was the last adult to see Elias alive—and has destroyed the security footage from that night.

---

7. “A.R.” – The Diary’s Author?
-------------------------------
Role: Unknown  
Age: Unknown  
Bio: Many diary pages are signed "A.R." with looping cursive. Some think it's Elias. Others believe it predates even him. Whoever A.R. is, they seem to know Maya’s every step.  
Secret: One of the pages contains Maya’s birth date, circled, with the words: *“The loop begins here.”*
