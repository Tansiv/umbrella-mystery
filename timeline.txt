Timeline – The Umbrella in the Rain: A Diary of Secrets
========================================================

June 14 – The Umbrella Appears
-------------------------------
A black umbrella is found in Maya's locker after a thunderstorm, dripping wet despite a sunny morning. Tucked beneath the strap is a torn diary page—signed by Elias.

June 15 – The Voice Message
----------------------------
Maya receives a voicemail from an unknown number. The voice is unmistakably Elias. The timestamp: 2:47 AM, two days after his funeral.

June 18 – The Rain Returns
---------------------------
A thunderstorm sweeps through Rowan School. During the power outage, someone rearranges the library shelves to spell out "SEEK THE WITNESS."

June 21 – The Photograph
-------------------------
Camellia develops a film roll from their last school trip. In one frame, a shadowy figure in a hat stands behind Elias—unnoticed at the time of shooting.

June 24 – The Red Ink Letter
-----------------------------
A blood-red envelope is slipped under Maya’s bedroom door. Inside: a folded page from Elias’s diary, a crossword puzzle filled with code, and the phrase: “The river never forgets.”

June 28 – The First Clue Breaks
-------------------------------
Maya and Cam decipher the crossword to reveal a location—Rowan Bell Tower. There they find an old typewriter and a cassette labeled “For M.”

July 2 – The Rainfall Incident
-------------------------------
The hallway ceiling collapses during a sudden downpour. Among the debris: a second umbrella with Elias’s initials stitched inside and a lockbox containing a key labeled “R.L.”

July 4 – The Confession
------------------------
Mrs. Lera admits to having tutored Elias in secret before his disappearance. She hands Maya a sealed envelope and tells her not to open it until “the seventh rainfall.”

July 8 – The Locked Diary
--------------------------
Hidden in a hollow space behind Mrs. Lera’s bookshelf, Maya finds Elias’s full diary—pages written in reverse, in ink that only appears in moonlight.

July 14 – The Final Puzzle
---------------------------
Elias’s diary ends with a map, a riddle, and a name: “A.R.” The riddle leads Maya back to the place it all began—the bridge over Stillwater River.

July 17 – The Umbrella in the Rain
-----------------------------------
As the rain falls again, Maya waits by the bridge. The Man in the Hat appears—holding an umbrella. He says, “You’ve almost found him.”  
He disappears. Maya opens the umbrella—and inside the canopy is a mirror, reflecting her own face… and someone standing behind her.
